What crossed the sky

SPHEREx's pipeline flags satellite streaks as unusable. Each streak is recorded by two cameras at the same moment; we recover the light at its edges and compare the cameras. Stars in the same images are the control.

Data: NASA SPHEREx (DR1, QR3) via NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive. Sky map: Aladin Lite (CDS). NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Planet X and SPHEREx.
